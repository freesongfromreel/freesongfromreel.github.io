About

Reel to Song Finder is a free online tool that identifies the music playing in any public video link — Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube, YouTube Shorts, X/Twitter and Facebook clips.

If you've ever watched a reel that simply says "original audio", you know the frustration of not being able to find the track. This tool looks past that label: it reads the audio from the link and matches it against a music catalogue to return the track name, the artist, and a link to hear the full song.

It's built as a simple, privacy-respecting utility: paste a link, get a song, move on.
