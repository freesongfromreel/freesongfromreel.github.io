Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

What we collect

When you paste a video link, the tool reads that link and fetches the post's audio solely to identify the song. The URL and the audio are used once, in memory, and are not stored, logged, sold or shared.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense). These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits. To learn more or opt out, please visit the relevant ad network's opt-out page. We do not control these cookies and are not responsible for them.

Third-party services

Song identification is performed by matching the audio against a third-party music catalogue. That catalogue may receive the audio clip for the sole purpose of producing a match. No other personal information is transmitted.
