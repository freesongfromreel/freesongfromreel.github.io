💡 Which links work?

Tip: paste a public link, not a private/post login-gated one. Reels shared from the app's share button usually work best. The tool reads the post's audio and matches it against Shazam's catalog to return the track, artist and a streaming link.

📖 How it works

What it does

Paste the link to any video with music — an Instagram Reel, a TikTok, a YouTube video or Short, or an X/Twitter clip — and the tool extracts its audio and identifies the song. You get the track name, the artist, and a link to hear it on Spotify or other services. All in a few seconds, no app, no account.

Why use it

Instagram often labels a reel simply “original audio” — leaving you no way to find the song. This tool looks past that label and matches the actual sound to identify it, even when the platform hides the track name.

Privacy

Your link isn't stored. The audio is read once, matched, and then thrown away. Nothing is sold or shared. See the privacy policy for details.
