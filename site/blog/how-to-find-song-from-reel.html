How to Find Any Song from a Reel: Step-by-Step

October 2026 · Free Song from Reel

Three methods, from fastest to most reliable. Start with the first; move down the list only if a platform blocks automated access.

Method 1: Paste the link (10–20 seconds)

  1. Open the reel/video and copy its link (on Instagram: share → Copy link; TikTok: share → Copy link; YouTube: share → copy URL).
  2. Go to freesongfromreel.github.io and paste it into the box.
  3. Press Find song. The tool reads the post's audio and returns the title, artist and streaming links.

Best with public links from the app's share menu. Private accounts, login-walled posts and expired stories can't be read. If the platform blocks the automated read (you may see “bot-check” or “blocked”), skip to Method 2.

Method 2: Save the video and upload it

  1. Save the video to your phone: Instagram has a Save video option in the share menu, Facebook has Download video, and for YouTube/X use your phone's screen recording — 10–15 seconds is enough. Full per-platform steps: help page.
  2. On the site, click 📁 File or drag & drop the video into the box.
  3. The tool extracts the audio, identifies the song and shows you the result.

This is the most reliable method because the file comes from your phone — no platform block stands between you and the audio.

Method 3: Record with the Android app (one tap)

  1. Install Free Song Recorder (APK, free).
  2. Open the video in your app of choice and start a recording in Free Song Recorder — it captures the exact audio you hear, even when the share link is cut short or the video can't be downloaded.
  3. Tap identify; title, artist and streaming links appear.

This is the one for reels that won't share, save or download — you always have the audio in front of you, so you can always capture it.

Pro tips

  • Longer is better: 15+ seconds of clean music matches far more reliably than 3 seconds with talking over it.
  • Expect the occasional miss: obscure remixes, live recordings and brand-new releases may not be in the recognition database. You get a clear “no match” rather than a wrong guess.
  • Verify the result: use the ▶ Play a sample link on the result card to preview the track and confirm it's the one before you head to Spotify or Apple Music.
