📥 How to download the video

If a link doesn't work (some platforms block automated access), download the video on your own device and drop the file into Free Song from Reel. Every platform below has a simple way to save a video — these are the official buttons, plus screen recording as a universal fallback.

📱 Android app: record the video right on your phone, then identify the song in one tap — download Free Song Recorder (APK, free).

Instagram TikTok YouTube X / Twitter Facebook

Instagram (Reels & videos)

  1. Open the reel or video in the Instagram app.
  2. Tap the share icon (paper plane) at the bottom.
  3. Tap Save video (or "Save reel"). It saves to your phone's gallery/Camera Roll.
  4. Can't see the save option? Use screen recording (below).

TikTok

  1. Open the video in the TikTok app.
  2. Tap Share (the arrow on the right side).
  3. Tap Save video. It saves to your gallery.

YouTube (videos & Shorts)

  1. YouTube has no official download button — use your phone's screen recorder:
  2. iPhone: Settings → Control Center → add Screen Recording, then record while the video plays.
  3. Android: swipe down twice → look for Screen record (varies by phone).
  4. Record just the first 10–15 seconds — that's enough to identify the song.

X / Twitter

  1. X has no official save button — use the same screen recording fallback (top 10–15 seconds is enough).
  2. Tap the video to widen it, start recording, play, stop.

Facebook

  1. Open the video.
  2. Tap the ⋯ (More) menu on the video.
  3. Tap Download video (public videos only; private ones can't be saved).

Get a screen recording onto this site

  1. After recording, the clip is in your gallery.
  2. On this site, click 📁 File at the top, or drag & drop the file into the box.
  3. The tool extracts the audio, identifies the song, and shows title, artist and a Spotify link — free.
